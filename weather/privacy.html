Who we are

Futuhima OÜ (registry code 17413077, Kesk-Ameerika tn 7-1, 10122 Tallinn, Estonia) makes the Finland x Weather show and runs the "X Weather" app. We are the data controller. Contact: tom.himanen@futuhima.ninja.

What the app does

"X Weather" is an internal tool. It uploads videos that Futuhima has produced to Futuhima's own YouTube channel and sets when they are published. Futuhima's own Google account is the only account that signs in to it.

What data it uses

  • Google user data: the app asks only for the youtube.upload permission. It uses that permission only to upload and schedule Futuhima's own videos. It does not read, collect, store or share any other data from Google or YouTube accounts.
  • The sign-in token that Google issues is stored only on Futuhima's own server, readable only by the publishing account. It is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Viewers: the app collects no data about people who watch the show. Watching happens on YouTube, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Weather data comes from Open-Meteo.com and contains no personal data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The sign-in token is kept until it is revoked. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. If you have any questions, or want something deleted, email us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee).
