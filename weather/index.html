Finland x Weather

A daily weather show by Futuhima OÜ

Finland x Weather is a daily weather forecast for Finland in English, presented by an AI presenter, Xena Rita. Every show is also a small fashion show: she wears a new look each day.

The show is produced on Futuhima's own hardware. The weather data comes from Open-Meteo.com. The finished show is published on Futuhima's YouTube channel. YouTube labels it as synthetic media.

The "X Weather" app

"X Weather" is Futuhima's internal publishing tool. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the finished shows to Futuhima's own YouTube channel and to schedule when they go public. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.
